About CardBaazi

CardBaazi brings Dehla Pakad, Spades, Call Break, Hearts, 3-2-5, Indian Rummy, Klondike, FreeCell, and Spider to iPhone. Practice with bots, follow seven 50-stage Academy journeys, find an online table, or invite friends to a private room.
