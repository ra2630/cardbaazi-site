Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I invite friends?
A: Tap "Create Room" on either Dehla Pakad or Hearts, then tap the "WhatsApp Invite" button to send an invite link to your friends.

Q: How does Dehla Pakad 2-in-a-row pile collection work?
A: In Dehla Pakad, cards accumulate in the center pile. When a team wins 2 consecutive tricks in a row, they collect all cards and 10s in the center pile!

Q: Does voice chat require an external server?
A: No! Voice chat uses direct low-latency peer-to-peer networking over your local network and Bluetooth.
