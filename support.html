CardBaazi Support

Help for gameplay, worldwide tables, accounts, credits, purchases, ads, and player safety.

Worldwide and private tables

Choose a game, then select Play with Bots, Find a Table, or Private Table. Private-table creators receive a room code to share. Epic Online Services connects players worldwide; no local Wi-Fi or Bluetooth mode is used.

Online credits are charged only when the first hand is dealt: 5 credits for the table creator and 1 credit for a joining player. Waiting in or leaving a lobby before the deal is free.

Teams and bots

When a team-game roster is full, each human chooses their own team. The table creator can drag bots between teams or remove a bot completely. A connected human cannot be removed by the host.

Credits, purchases, and offer codes

Every newly signed-in account receives 100 starting credits. Offline bot play and Solitaire are free. Academy stages 1–10 in each game are free; later stages unlock in blocks of five.

Credit packs are consumable in-app purchases processed by Apple. Credits do not expire, cannot be transferred, and have no cash value. Open Profile → Credits & purchases to view available packs or redeem an official App Store offer code. Purchase issues can also be reviewed through your Apple purchase history.

Optional rewarded ads

When more credits are needed, you may choose an optional rewarded video. A reward is granted only after the ad provider confirms completion. Daily limits apply, and availability varies by country and inventory. Ad privacy choices appear when required.

Chat, voice, blocking, and reports

Open table chat and tap the shield to mute voice, block communication, or report a player. Blocking hides chat, reactions, and voice without affecting cards or scoring. Harassment, hateful content, threats, spam, cheating, and offensive player names are not allowed.

Account deletion

Open your profile and select Delete Account & Reset Data to remove local profile, progress, statistics, credit state, and safety preferences. For assistance with service-side information, email support.
